I am a **PhD candidate at Télécom Paris** (LTCI lab., IDS Department, S2A team), supervised by **Charlotte Laclau**, **Tiphaine Viard** and **Bruno Sguerra**. My thesis, *Predicting Edges, Constraining Edges: From Fairness in Link Prediction to Edge-Girth Realizability*, is part of the project *REFAIR: Revisiting the Foundations of Algorithmic Fairness for Graphs* and is conducted in partnership with **Deezer Research**. My defense is planned for **January 2027**.

My research spans several areas:

* **Graph machine learning:** fairness, link prediction, expressivity of GNN
* **Graph theory and combinatorics:** cycles, edge-girth
* **Music recommendation and user modeling**
